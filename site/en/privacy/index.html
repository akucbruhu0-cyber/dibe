Privacy

DIBE transcribes on your device. It has no analytics, no telemetry, no accounts and no update checks.

What stays on your Mac

  • Recordings, imported media, transcripts, summaries and settings (~/Library/Application Support/io.github.akucbruhu0-cyber.dibe and ~/Documents/DIBE).
  • Transcription itself: the vibe-server engine inside the app runs locally and listens only on 127.0.0.1, behind a token that changes every time it starts.

When DIBE uses the network: only when you ask

You choose to…DIBE contactsWhat is sent
Download a modelHugging Face or the Vibe project's GitHub releasesA normal file download
Transcribe a web linkThe website, and GitHub once to download yt-dlpThe link you entered
Use AI summaries or dictation clean-upThe provider you configured: Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), your OpenAI-compatible server, or Ollama (local by default)The transcript text being summarized or cleaned, with your API key
Record from your phoneThe pairing page hosted by the Vibe project, and peer-to-peer relays (iroh)Audio travels encrypted from your phone to your Mac
Report a bugOpens a pre-filled GitHub issue in your browserOnly what you submit there

Nothing is sent in the background. With AI turned off and every model already downloaded, DIBE works completely offline.

Compared to Vibe

DIBE removed Vibe's anonymous usage analytics entirely (the Aptabase plugin and every event), and turned off automatic update checks.
